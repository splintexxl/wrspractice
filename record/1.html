About this solve

Nine-year-old Teodor Zajder from Poland shattered the three-second barrier at GLS Big Cubes 2026 in Gdańsk — becoming the first person in history to officially solve a 3×3 in under three seconds. The crowd erupted the moment the timer stopped at 2.76.

He noticed during inspection that the scramble was unusually generous, planning much of the solution before his hands ever touched the cube. The solve itself took only 29 moves, executed at over 10 turns per second.

Teodor had already made headlines in 2023 when he set the 2×2 world record at age seven. With 60+ competitions and dozens of gold medals to his name, he dethroned Xuanyi Geng's 3.05 from April 2025 — not by a hundredth, but by nearly three tenths.
