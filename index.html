Sub-3 barrier

For years, three seconds was considered the human ceiling. The first official solve under that mark rewrote what speedcubers thought was possible.

Move count

Elite singles often land between 25–35 moves. Efficiency — not just raw turning speed — separates a good solve from a world record.
